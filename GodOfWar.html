God of War Series

Game Creator

The God of War series was created by Santa Monica Studio and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment.

Game Background

The series begins with Kratos, a Spartan warrior who serves the Greek gods. He makes a deal with Ares, the God of War, to gain power. Ares tricks him into killing his own wife and daughter, which causes Kratos to seek revenge.

Kratos eventually kills Ares and becomes the new God of War, but he continues to suffer betrayal from the gods, especially Zeus. This leads him to wage war against Olympus.

In the newer games, Kratos moves into Norse mythology and tries to raise his son Atreus while escaping his violent past.

Why This Is My Favorite Game

The God of War series is my favorite because of its intense combat, smooth controls, and brutal fighting style. The gameplay is very satisfying, especially when using different weapons and abilities. I also enjoy how the story evolves over time, from Kratos seeking revenge to becoming a father trying to guide his son. The storytelling is emotional and powerful, which makes the series stand out from other action games.

Main Characters

  • Kratos – A Spartan warrior who becomes the God of War and seeks revenge after being betrayed, after seeking his revenge, he became a father once again and left his old self behind.
  • Atreus – Kratos’ son who travels with him in the Norse games.
  • Ares – The original God of War who tricks Kratos into killing his own family.
  • Zeus – King of the Greek gods, a major antagonist who betrays Kratos, and he's also Kratos father.
  • Athena – A goddess who guides Kratos but also plays a role in the gods’ manipulation.
  • Baldur – A Norse god and enemy in God of War (2018).
  • Thor – The Norse god of thunder featured in Ragnarök.
  • Odin – The main antagonist in Ragnarök.

God of War Timeline

2005 – God of War (PS2)

Ares tricks Kratos into killing his family, causing Kratos to obtain Pandora's box to get his revenge on Ares.

2007 – God of War II

Kratos, the new God of War, who is betrayed, stripped of his Godhood, and killed by Zeus after refusing to submit.

2010 – God of War III

Kratos destroys Olympus and kills all of the Olympians Gods.

2018 – God of War

Kratos living in the Norse realm of Midgard with his young son, Atreus. Going through the realms to scatter his wife ashes on the highest peak of all the realms

2022 – Ragnarök

Kratos and Atreus, faces Thor and Odin.
